## Name and corrections

The model name is `SEN_T_I_E_N_C_E`. In this repository it means a testable computational
construction; it does not certify subjective experience in a human, an AI, a microtubule, or a
claustrum.

The relevant scientist is **Roger Penrose**, not Robert Penrose. He shared the 2020 Nobel Prize in
Physics for work showing that black-hole formation follows from general relativity—not for MRI,
microtubules, or consciousness ([Nobel Prize facts](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2020/penrose/facts/)).
Penrose and Stuart Hameroff proposed Orch OR, in which quantum processes in neuronal microtubules
contribute to consciousness; it remains a proposed theory rather than established neuroscience
([Hameroff and Penrose, 2014](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24070914/)).

“Francis” is bound to **Francis Crick**. Crick and Christof Koch proposed that the claustrum could
coordinate information relevant to consciousness
([Crick and Koch, 2005](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16147522/)). Subsequent evidence is mixed:
human lesion results associated claustrum injury with duration but not frequency of loss of
consciousness ([Chau et al., 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26186439/)), while high-resolution
MRI has measured claustrum connectivity and task-switching activity without establishing it as a
single seat of consciousness
([Krimmel et al., 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6629463/)).

Microtubules have established classical cellular roles in neuronal transport, synapse formation,
and plasticity ([Lombino et al., 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38050126/)). A rat study found
that a microtubule-stabilizing drug delayed an anesthetic behavioral endpoint, but that result does
not isolate a quantum mechanism or establish human sentience
([Khan et al., 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39147581/)).

### 1. Relic spectral recurrence

Let a preregistered frequency `f_*` represent the first candidate landing. Relative landing
exponents are

```text

f_n = f_* 3^d_n.
```

For an observed peak `g_i`, use the log-three residual

```text
r_i = min_n |log_3(g_i/f_*) - d_n|.
T = sum_i 1[r_i <= epsilon].
```

Before looking at held-out spectra, freeze `f_*`, the frequency band, level count, peak detector,
and tolerance `epsilon`. Compare `T` against log-uniform surrogate spectra with the same peak count
and band. The implemented finite-sample permutation estimate is

```text
p = (1 + number of null scores >= T) / (1 + trials).
```

A non-small held-out `p`, sensitivity to minor preprocessing changes, or failure to replicate is
evidence against the recurrence mapping. A small `p` would show a ternary spectral pattern, not by
itself quantum computation or consciousness.
